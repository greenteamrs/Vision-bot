Ranks

Rank checks use days, LP, and community level for Visionaries members. Ranks with notifications off and temporary ranks are never promotion targets. Mark seasonal ranks as Temporary before syncing WOM: the bot saves the previous permanent rank and checks progression against it, even while WOM shows a higher seasonal rank. Permanent Discord rank roles can also identify the original rank. Gnome Child and Leader need a saved WOM rank because they have no separate rank role. Use /checkranks or Check Now for all outstanding promotions.
